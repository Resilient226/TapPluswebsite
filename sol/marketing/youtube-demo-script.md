# TAP+ Sol — First YouTube Demo

**Purpose:** Explain the product in under one minute. This is a demonstration, not a performance claim.

## Video title
TAP+ Sol Demo: Share Your Digital Profile With One Tap

## Format
- Length: 35–50 seconds
- Orientation: vertical (9:16), also export a horizontal crop if convenient
- Style: clean, close-up product demo; natural light; no stock testimonials or fabricated results
- Audio: voiceover or clear on-camera narration

## Shot list and voiceover

**0–4 sec — Hook**  
*Shot:* Hold the TAP+ Sol card next to a phone.  
*Voiceover:* “Ever meet someone who wants your work, your links, or your booking details—and then have to explain where to find everything?”

**4–10 sec — Show the card**  
*Shot:* Close-up of the physical card; show the TAP+ branding.  
*Voiceover:* “This is TAP+ Sol, a tap-to-share NFC card designed to connect an in-person introduction to a digital destination.”

**10–20 sec — Real tap test**  
*Shot:* Tap the actual card against a compatible phone. Record the actual destination opening. Do not fake the phone interaction in editing.  
*Voiceover:* “Tap the card with a compatible phone, and it opens the link configured for that card.”

**20–31 sec — Show a real profile**  
*Shot:* Screen recording of the real, working digital destination. Show only features that are currently implemented and tested.  
*Voiceover:* “That destination can help you share the digital information you choose to make available. Check your setup before handing the card to someone.”

**31–41 sec — Audience use case**  
*Shot:* Use the card in a mock introduction at a studio, event, or work setting.  
*Voiceover:* “For DJs, artists, photographers, and independent professionals, it’s a simple physical way to point a new connection toward your online presence.”

**41–48 sec — CTA**  
*Shot:* Card close-up with the TAP+ Sol URL on screen.  
*Voiceover:* “Explore TAP+ Sol at tapplus.top/sol. The ordering link will be added once checkout is ready.”
