Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is TAP+ Sol?

TAP+ Sol is a physical NFC card intended to open a linked digital destination when tapped with a compatible phone.

Do people need to download an app?

The intended experience opens a web destination from the NFC tap. Device behavior can vary, so test the card with the phones your customers are likely to use before launch.

Can I share my portfolio or booking links?

The card points to its configured digital destination. The links and profile options available depend on the current profile setup; confirm the exact options before ordering.

Is this the same as TAP+ Hospitality?

No. TAP+ Sol is the individual tap-to-share card offer. TAP+ Hospitality is a separate product for restaurants and hospitality businesses.
